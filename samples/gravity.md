# Gravity

A one-page lesson: two ideas about gravity, each with its common mistake.

## Why things fall

Gravity pulls every object toward the center of the Earth. Without air resistance, a heavy ball and a light ball dropped together hit the ground at the same time.

Common mistake: "Heavy things fall faster than light things." Without air resistance, heavy and light objects fall at the same rate.

## Gravity in space

Gravity does not stop in space. It is what keeps the Moon going around the Earth.

Common mistake: "There is no gravity in space." Gravity keeps pulling on things far out in space, like the Moon.
